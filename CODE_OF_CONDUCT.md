# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Pinshot contributors are expected to create a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or affiliation.

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being considerate, constructive, and professional.
- Assuming good intent while addressing technical disagreement directly.
- Giving and receiving feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and helping repair their impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information without consent, and any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-related communication. It also applies when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove or edit contributions, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate for violations.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the repository owner through [GitHub](https://github.com/r4khul). Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially to the extent practical. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not permitted.
